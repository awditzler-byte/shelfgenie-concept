Talk with ShelfGenie ↗
Do I need to replace my cabinets?+

Our custom pull-out shelves are designed to fit inside your existing cabinets. Your designer will assess your space and recommend suitable solutions. Custom closet systems follow a separate design process.

What happens at the free consultation?+

A ShelfGenie designer gets to know your space, what you store and what you would like to improve. Together, you explore a custom solution and a personalized quote. Ask your local team about consultation formats and timing.

How much does a custom solution cost?+

Your investment depends on your project, dimensions, number of pull-outs, materials and selections. Your designer prepares a quote around your specific design. Ask your local ShelfGenie about available financing options and terms.

Can you help make storage easier to reach?+

Custom pull-out shelves bring cabinet contents forward, making them easier to see and reach. Your designer can help you consider solutions around your routines and access needs.

What warranty is available?+

Warranty coverage depends on the product line and applicable terms. Ask your designer to explain the coverage for your selections and review the current ShelfGenie warranty.

Are all solutions available in my area?+

ShelfGenie locations are independently owned and operated. Available services, materials and scheduling vary by market. Your local team will confirm the options for your home.
